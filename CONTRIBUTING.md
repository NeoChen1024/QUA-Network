# Contributing

## Licence of contributions

A contribution is made under the same licences as the part of the repository
it changes, as listed in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), and under the patent
terms there: the project's own non-assertion statement and the OWF
Contributor License Agreement 1.0 (Copyright and Patent). Nothing is assigned to any person or organisation: the
rights are granted to everyone at the time of the contribution and do not
depend on the project or its maintainers continuing to exist.

## Sign-off

Every commit carries a `Signed-off-by` line (`git commit -s`). It certifies
the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/):
that you wrote the contribution or otherwise have the right to submit it
under these licences. The sign-off is also how you state your agreement to
the terms above; no separate signed form is collected.

## Third-party material

Do not copy text, tables or code from sources whose licence does not allow
it. This includes the APRS protocol reference, which permits non-commercial
copying only, and the AX.25 specification. Describe the behaviour in your
own words and cite the source. Third-party code is referenced through a
submodule or a recorded commit, never pasted in.
